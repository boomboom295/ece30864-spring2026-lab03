(a) What are some pros and cons of Agile development (vs non-Agile)? List 2 of each.
    One big pro is flexibility. Agile development allows individuals to work in small groups and contribute equally to a project without having to worry about overlapping code and working on the same issue.
    Great when requirements are uncertain and allows stakeholders to see progress and easier for individuals to collaborate with eachother.
    One con is that it is difficult to figure out how much to allocate to the project. Things like time or money are difficult to allocate.
    It is difficult to manage longer/larger projects because Agile is mainly focused on short term effects.
(b) Explain the roles and responsibilites of the Scrum Master vs the Project Owner
    The product owner determines what to build whereas the Scrum Master determines how it is built. The scrum masters can add blocks and create issues that need to be worked on.
(c) What are the 3 types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle? List at least 2 points on what needs to be discussed during each of these meetings.
    Sprint Planning
    - Determine the goals and Scope of the project.
    - Break down backlog items into tasks
    Daily Scrum
    - Team members share what they have been working on.
    - Determine blocks or bottlenecks
    Sprint Review
    - Demo the project
    - Reflects on past stuff to improve
(d) Differentiate between Epic Story Bug and Task issue types in Jira
    Epic
    - A large amount of work that contains stories and tasks and spans between multiple sprints
    Story
    - A feature meant for the user that is meant to be completed in one sprint
    Bug
    - Issue in software that the devs need to fix 
    Task
    - A specific thing that devs need to work on
